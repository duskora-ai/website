From backlog to production

Each ticket moves through the same stages a good engineering team follows. A dedicated agent owns each step, and every step leaves a trail you can read.

01

Plan

Reads the ticket and your codebase, then writes a plan before any code changes.

02

Build

03

Review

A separate agent reviews the diff and sends it back if it isn't right.

04

Tests every acceptance criterion and attaches screenshots and recordings as evidence.

05

What counts as a ticket?

A ticket is counted once, when an agent first starts building it. Rework and retries are included, up to a fair-use limit of 10 runs per ticket.

Who pays for model costs?

You bring your own Anthropic, OpenAI or Google key. Model usage is billed by that vendor, not by Duskora.

What happens at the limit?

New tickets wait until the next period, or you can upgrade.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time, from Billing. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.
