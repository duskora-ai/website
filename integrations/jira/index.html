Duskora with Jira

Duskora works from your existing board. It syncs two ways with Jira, so your team keeps planning in Jira while agents plan, build, review, test and deploy the work. You stay in control of what goes live.

Two-way sync with Jira

Label an issue in Jira and it joins the Duskora backlog. As agents work on it, progress flows back to Jira automatically, so the issue shows where the work is without anyone updating it by hand.

From Jira issue to pull request

A planning agent reads the ticket and your codebase, then writes a plan before any code changes. The build agent implements the plan on its own branch, runs your CI checks and opens a pull request on GitHub, checked against the same CI your team already trusts.

Reviewed and tested by separate agents

A different agent reviews the diff and sends it back to build if it isn't right. QA then tests every acceptance criterion and attaches screenshots and recordings as evidence. Every step leaves a trail you can read.

Nothing deploys without your approval

Every change waits at an approval gate. Approve, request changes or retry from the app or straight from Slack. After your approval, Duskora merges and watches the deploy through to production.
