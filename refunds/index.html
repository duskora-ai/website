Refunds and Cancellation

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Plans

Duskora.ai is available on monthly or annual plans. You can cancel at any time. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

Cancellation

Organisation owners cancel from Settings → Billing → Manage billing. Your plan keeps working until the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again. You can undo the cancellation from the same page before then. After the period ends, agents stop running, but your projects, tickets and settings stay in place. You can still sign in, and you can subscribe again at any time to carry on where you left off. If you want your data deleted, email us.

The free trial needs no card, so it never turns into a paid plan by itself. When it ends, agents stop until you choose a plan.

You can switch plans or billing intervals from the same page. Stripe shows the amount you'll be charged or credited before you confirm.

Refunds

Payments aren't refundable, and we don't refund the unused part of a billing period, except in the following cases:

  • Annual plans: if you ask within 14 days of your first annual payment, we'll refund it in full.
  • Our mistakes: we'll refund duplicate charges, charges made after you cancelled, and charges for a plan you didn't choose.
  • Where the law requires it.

Model usage on your own Anthropic, OpenAI or Google keys is billed by those vendors, not by us, so we can't refund it.

To ask for a refund, email hello@duskora.ai from an owner's address, giving your organisation name. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.
